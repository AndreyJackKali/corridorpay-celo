02 · Real User & Broken Status Quo

Paying Distributed Contractors Shouldn't Cost $160 or Expose Keys

Named User: Andrey, Operations Lead at Veridian Distributed Studio, paying 5 remote engineers & analysts across Brazil, Argentina, Nigeria, Colombia, and Indonesia from a single USD₮ treasury.

Traditional Wire Rails
  • $30–$45 SWIFT fee + 3–5% bank FX markup (~$160 per 5-person cycle)
  • 3–5 business day settlement delays & failed intermediary hops
  • Manual spreadsheet reconciliation every payroll window
Naive AI Agent Wallets
  • Handing a raw EOA private key to an AI agent = total treasury loss risk
  • No on-chain daily cap, no contract allowlist, no kill switch
  • No verifiable attribution or on-chain agent identity
